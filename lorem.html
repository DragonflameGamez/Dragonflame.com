What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem ipsum is a standard placeholder text used in the printing and typesetting industry.

History

While lorem ipsum may seem to be random or have no meaning it comes from a Latin text.

Lorem Ipsum comes from all the way back to 45 BC, specifically from sections 1.10.32 and 1.10.33 of "de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" by Cicero.

'Lorem ipsum' itself has no meaning but it is a cut version of the phrase 'Delorem ipsum' which is Latin for 'Pain itself'.

Why is it used now and why was it cut down?

In the 16th century (1500s), an unknown printer needed dummy text to demonstrate font typefaces. To prevent readers from being distracted by the content, they took the original writings of Cicero and scrambled the words and removed letters.

Modern Usage 1960s

Lorem ipsum was unused for several centuries until it was popularized in the 1960s by Letraset, a company that produced carbon paper and other office supplies.

Digital usage

Today we use Lorem Ipsum in digital design and web development as a placeholder for text you have not yet written. Lorem Ipsum should be used to demonstrate the visual form of a document or a typeface without relying on meaningful content.
